How do I put Jibo in RCM mode?
  1. Fully shut Jibo down.
  2. Open the hatch on the back of his head to reach the round button panel.
  3. Connect the USB cable from your computer to Jibo's micro-USB port under the same hatch.
  4. Press and hold the RCM button (the lower of the two small buttons). Keep holding it while you turn Jibo back on with his power button, then let go of the RCM button.
  5. In RCM mode, the red light on his forehead stays on and you don't hear his startup chime. If he chimes or starts normally, shut him down and try again.
